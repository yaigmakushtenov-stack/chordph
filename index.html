About Chord.ph

Built by Filipino worship musicians, for worship musicians.

Chord.ph started with a very ordinary Sunday problem: chord charts scattered across screenshots, group chats, notebooks and half a dozen apps — none of them made for a band playing together. We wanted one clean place where the whole team reads the same sheet, in the right key, on any device.

So we built it. Instant transpose and capo shapes, Nashville numbers, setlists and medleys, an on-device key & BPM analyzer, and band sync so your worship team shares one library and one setlist that updates live. It runs in the browser, works offline, and installs to your home screen — no account needed to start.

Chord.ph is free for every musician. Team features and a few power tools support the project so the core stays free and keeps getting better — made in the Philippines, for the worldwide worship community.

Terms & privacy

The short, plain version.

Using Chord.ph

Chord.ph is provided free, as-is, for worship and personal music practice. We do our best to keep it running and accurate, but we can't guarantee it's error-free or always available. Use it at your own discretion for your ministry and practice.

Chords & charts (disclaimer)

Chord charts and tabs are contributed by the community as interpretations to help people play and worship. They are not official transcriptions. All songs remain the property of their respective songwriters, artists and publishers. If you own a work and want a chart removed, contact us and we'll take it down.

Your data & privacy

You can use the app with no account at all — your songs and setlists stay on your device. If you sign in (to sync across devices or use band features), we store your email and your library in our secure database (Supabase) purely to provide the service. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Analysis in the Key & BPM Analyzer runs on your device; audio you add to the practice player stays on your device.

Contact

Questions, takedowns, or partnership: use the contact form above. This is a plain-language summary and a work in progress, not formal legal advice.
